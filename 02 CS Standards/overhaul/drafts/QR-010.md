# QR-010 Where things are in the app

Describes the FEB Composites app at feb-composites.web.app as the code stood on 2026-10-10. Tab and button names are copied from the files above. Where a standard says how to do the work, this page only says where the button is.

## 1. What tabs does the app have?

The sidebar lists twelve tabs: Dashboard and Season at the top; Parts, Work Orders, Molds, Inventory and R&D under Build; Schedule and Budget under Planning; Documents, Reports and People under Team (`app/core.js` TABS, GROUPS).

Also called: app, web app, FEB Composites, the website, tabs, pages, menu, sidebar, nav, navigation.

| Group | Tab | What it is for (from its tooltip) |
|---|---|---|
| (top) | Dashboard | The team-wide picture |
| (top) | Season | The blueprint: every part we mean to make |
| Build | Parts | Every part, its mold, its stack and its runs |
| Build | Work Orders | One run at making a part |
| Build | Molds | Molds, stack plans and tooling board |
| Build | Inventory | What we have and where it lives |
| Build | R&D | Coupon studies, test pieces and trials |
| Planning | Schedule | The season by station, or the week by person |
| Planning | Budget | Purchases, arrivals and reimbursement |
| Team | Documents | Datasheets, standards, printables |
| Team | Reports | Exports, print boards, labels |
| Team | People | The roster and who carries what |

On a phone the sidebar is behind the Menu button at the top left, or swipe in from the left edge. On a wide screen, Collapse at the foot of the sidebar shrinks it to icons.

Build runs in the order the work happens: you make the part, then the run that makes it, pulled off a mold, from material in Inventory (`app/core.js`).

## 2. Where is tooling board? (Stock is gone)

Tooling board is under Inventory → Boards, one record per sheet or offcut (`BRD-SN6-###`). There is no Stock tab any more: old Stock links and scanned board codes open Inventory or Molds instead (`app/core.js`, `app/inventory.js`).

Also called: tooling board, board, foam board, Precision Board, PBLT, offcuts, remnants, Stock, stock tab, rack, board rack.

1. Open Inventory.
2. Press Boards in the row at the top (Storage map, Items list, Materials list, Boards).
3. Press + Board to record a new sheet or offcut.

The stack planner picks thicknesses only from boards recorded here, so a board that is not in Boards does not exist to the planner (CS-003 §5).

Stack plans and the cut list live on the Molds tab, not here (QR-010 §3).

## 3. Where are molds, stack plans and the cut list?

Everything about molds is on the Molds tab: + Mold to plan a new one, Cut list, Labels and Plan from Fusion along the top, and the list of molds with their stage. A mold's stack plan (`STK-SN6-###`) shows under Mold file on the mold's page (`app/molds.js`).

Also called: mold, mould, tool, molds tab; stack plan, board plan, stock plan, STK; cut list, cutting list; mold stage.

| What you want | Where |
|---|---|
| Plan a new mold's stack | Molds → + Mold |
| The cut list for blanks | Molds → Cut list (shows once a stack plan exists) |
| Mold labels | Molds → Labels |
| The Fusion add-in download | Molds → Plan from Fusion (QR-011) |
| A mold's current plan | The mold's page → Mold file; older plans under Earlier, marked Superseded |
| Molds by stage | The stage filter (All stages) above the list |

The filter chips "retired" and "no home" show retired molds and molds with no location.

A mold that will not be used again is retired, never deleted (CS-003 §4).

## 4. Where are resin, cloth and consumables?

On Inventory. Storage map shows every shelf and what is on it; Items list and Materials list show the same things as lists. Receive a delivery, Labels, Custom label and Export sit on the same toolbar (`app/inventory.js`).

Also called: inventory, materials, resin, hardener, cloth, fabric, carbon, consumables, jugs, rolls, shelf, storage, find, location.

| What you want | Where |
|---|---|
| What is on a shelf | Inventory → Storage map, then the shelf |
| Resin, hardener, fabric and consumable lots as a list | Inventory → Materials list |
| Panels, jigs and storage locations as a list | Inventory → Items list |
| Log a delivery | Inventory → Receive a delivery (QR-006) |
| What is on order and not yet here | The Incoming card on the Storage map |
| A new shelf or bin | Storage map → + Location |
| Print labels for many records | Inventory → Labels |
| The EH&S reconciliation | Inventory → Export |
| Import from campus RSS | Inventory → EH&S import (leads only) |

There used to be separate Items and Materials tabs. Since 2026-08-04 they are views of Inventory, and old links to them land there (`app/core.js`).

To find one thing fast, scan its label with the scan button in the top bar, or search with Search (⌘K).

## 5. Where did Tickets, Items, Materials, Stock and Weekly Plan go?

They are hidden now, and old links still work. Items and Materials are views of Inventory. Stock opens Inventory → Boards or the Molds tab. Weekly Plan is the Week by person view of Schedule. Tickets became issues on a work order (`app/core.js` TABS).

Also called: tickets, projects, PROJ, items tab, materials tab, stock tab, weekly plan, week plan, missing tab, find, location, lost tab, moved, renamed, hidden tab.

| Old tab | Where it is now |
|---|---|
| Items | Inventory → Items list |
| Materials | Inventory → Materials list |
| Stock | Inventory → Boards for board; Molds for stack plans |
| Weekly Plan | Schedule → Week by person |
| Tickets | An issue on the run it holds up, in the run's Issues section (QR-007) |

The Tickets tab was shelved on 2026-08-25, not deleted. Every `PROJ-SN6-###` record is still in the database, and a link to one still opens its page (`app/core.js`).

Schedule has two views at the top: Season by station and Week by person (`app/timeline.js`).

## 6. Where is Recently deleted?

On the Reports tab, always open, under Recently deleted. Press Restore on a row to put it back. A deleted record stays for 30 days with its uploads (`app/reports.js`; CS-013 §7.7).

Also called: recently deleted, deleted, bin, trash, recycle bin, undo delete, restore, recover; archived.

Each row says what was deleted, who deleted it, how long ago and how many days are left. Restoring one brings back everything deleted with it, such as a run and its issues.

Past 30 days a lead presses Empty them, and the records are gone for good (CS-013 §7.7 step 4).

An archived part or run is not in Recently deleted. It is hidden from the lists but kept whole: press the archived chip on the Parts or Work Orders list to see archived records, each with Restore (CS-013 §7.7 step 1).

The rest of the Reports tab, top to bottom: the weekly status board (Print status board), Layup techniques, and Exports and maintenance, which holds Export a CSV, Labels (Label sheet, Custom label, Label media) and, for leads, Maintenance (`app/reports.js`).

## 7. Where are the standards and datasheets?

Datasheets (TDS and SDS) are on the Documents tab, under Datasheets. The CS standards are not listed on the Documents tab today: ask Paul, who searches them and links the section he quotes (`app/documents.js`, `app/paul.js`).

Also called: standards, CS standards, SOP, procedure, quick reference, QR; datasheet, TDS, SDS, MSDS, safety sheet; documents, docs, files.

1. For a datasheet, open Documents and look under Datasheets, or type its name in "search documents…".
2. For a resin or hardener you are holding, open its lot page. The TDS and SDS buttons there open that material's sheets (CS-014 §5).
3. For a standard, press Ask Paul in the top bar and ask the question. Each answer lists its Sources; press one to open the standard beside the chat.

The top of Documents is the Team shelf: links pinned with + Pin a link, for the things people keep asking for (the master tracker, the meeting deck). + Upload document adds a file of your own (`app/documents.js`).

The standards files are still shipped with the app, so a standard that was issued stays retrievable. They are only left off the Documents list (`app/documents.js`).

## 8. How do I ask Paul or Steve?

Press Ask Paul (the speech-bubble button in the top bar). It shows only when a lead has AI features on. The panel has a Paul and Steve switch, Chats for your saved conversations, and New chat (`app/paul.js`, `app/core.js`).

Also called: Paul, ask Paul, chat, AI, assistant, helper, bot; Steve, Fibreglast; chats, chat history, new chat.

1. Press Ask Paul in the top bar.
2. Type the question. If a record is open, Paul starts from it ("Looking at …"); press × on that chip to ask without it.
3. Press a number in the answer, or a line under Sources, to open where it came from.
4. Press Chats to see saved conversations, and Back to return.
5. Press New chat to start over.

Paul answers from the team's records, the standards and the datasheets, and cites each one. Steve is the team's joke helper: every Steve answer carries "Steve is always wrong" and an Ask Paul instead button. Under a Paul answer, "What would Steve say?" asks Steve (`app/paul.js`).

Never act on a number from Steve. Check any number from Paul against the source he links.

## 9. The top bar: scan, search, notifications and backup

The top bar holds Scan a label, Search (⌘K), Ask Paul, Notifications and the light or dark theme switch. On a wide screen it also shows the version with What's new, Backup, and Sign out. On a phone those are under More (⋯) (`app/core.js`).

Also called: top bar, header, scan, scanner, QR scanner, search, find, notifications, bell, backup, sign out, log out, version, what's new.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Scan a label | Opens the camera; reads an FEB label or a UC EH&S tag and opens the record (CS-001 §7.12) |
| Search (⌘K) | Searches parts, work orders, people and documents |
| Ask Paul | Opens Paul, the app's helper (QR-010 §8) |
| Notifications | Your notifications, with a count of unread ones. Not shown to a guest |
| Backup / Backup database | Downloads the whole database as one file |
| What's new | What changed in this version |

On a phone, More (⋯) holds Dark theme or Light theme, Change photo, Backup database, Sign out and the version line. Leads also see Restore from backup and the ✨ AI features switch with the month's spend (`app/core.js`).

A guest (View as guest) can read everything and change nothing, and sees Sign in instead of Backup.
