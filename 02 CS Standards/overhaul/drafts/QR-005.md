## 1. Which label goes on what?

Mold: back face, engraved ID plus a 5522 label. Part: trim area at demould, then a hidden face. Chemical container with a UC EH&S tag: no FEB label. Shelf: front edge. Mixed pot: tape and marker (CS-001 §7.2).

Also called: label, sticker, tag; QR, QR code, barcode; where does the label go, labelling, labeling.

| Object | Where it goes | Media (CS-001 §7.2) |
|---|---|---|
| Mold | Back face, at least 25 mm inboard of the outline. Never the working surface or the flange | Engraved ID plus a printed 5522 label |
| Part, at demould | Trim area, bag side, inside the CAD trim line | Printed, permanent adhesive |
| Part, after trim | A hidden face, 25 mm or more from any DP420 bond line | 5522, before clear coat |
| Test panel | Bag side, in the offcut margin; coupon IDs marked across the cut lines | Printed plus paint marker |
| Tensile coupon | Paint marker on the tab only; the `CPN` label goes on its bag or tag | Marker plus printed label |
| R&D study | The bag, tray or box its coupons live in | Printed |
| Fabric roll | A tag through the core, on wire or a zip tie | Printed label on the tag |
| Fabric offcut bag | On the bag, with the remaining length in paint marker beside it | Printed plus marker |
| Chemical container with an EH&S tag | No FEB label; record the tag in EH&S barcode | Campus tag |
| Chemical container with no tag yet | On the container | 5522 only |
| Consumable (cups, bag film, tape) | The box or bin, one label per record | Printed |
| Tooling board sheet | Paint marker on the face, 15 mm characters | Marker |
| Board remnant | ID in paint marker plus the printed board label on a taped tag | Marker plus tag |
| Storage location | Front edge of the shelf, readable standing up | Printed |
| Jig, fixture | A non-clamping face | Printed |

Issues, purchases, documents and stack plans never get a label (CS-001 §2).

## 2. Where does the label go on a mold?

On the back face, at least 25 mm inboard of the outline on every side, and never on the working surface or within 40 mm of the flange perimeter on either face (CS-001 §6 rules 1 and 2; §7.2). The printed label goes on only after sealing is finished (CS-001 §7.4).

Also called: mold label, mould label, tool ID, engraving, engraved ID, keep-out zone.

A mold is identified three times (CS-001 §7.4):

1. At glue-up, write the mold ID (`MOLD-SN6-###`), the part name, the WO and an up arrow in paint marker, 15 mm characters, on two adjacent side faces of the glued stack.
2. At machining, engrave the mold ID on the back face: 6 mm cap height, 1.0 mm deep, single-line font, 3.175 mm ball or 90° V-bit. The engraving is the record of record.
3. At the end of sealing, after the fourth wax coat is buffed and the PVA is dry, scuff a patch of the back face to 220 grit.
4. Wipe the patch with IPA.
5. Apply the printed 5522 label to the patch.

Why the keep-outs: anything on the tool face telegraphs into the part, and tacky tape is 12.7 mm wide, so a label edge under the seal is a leak channel (CS-001 §6).

> Do not apply the printed label before sealing. It gets brushed over with XCR, or peeled off leaving adhesive XCR will not wet out (CS-001 §7.4).

When a mold is stripped and re-sealed, print a new label with the new seal date and parts-pulled count (CS-001 §7.4).

## 3. Labeling a part at demould (demold): why two labels?

At demould, print two identical labels on 5522. Label A goes on the trim area, bag side, inside the CAD trim line. Label B is stapled to the work order traveler and goes on a hidden face after sanding (CS-001 §7.5).

Also called: demould, demold, de-mould; labeling, labelling; part label, part sticker; traveler, traveller.

Do not peel and re-stick a label. A label peeled off a demoulded part carries wax and PVA on its adhesive (CS-001 §7.5).

1. At demould, print two identical labels on 5522.
2. Apply label A to the trim area on the bag side, inside the CAD trim line.
3. Staple label B to the work order traveler.
4. At trim, label A is cut off with the trim and binned. The traveler now carries the part's identity.
5. After sanding and before clear coat, apply label B to the scuffed, IPA-wiped hidden face, 25 mm or more from any DP420 bond line (CS-001 §7.2).
6. Clear coat goes over label B where the geometry allows.

On a sheet the app prints one label per record, so the second label is a second print. On a roll, Copies of each set to 2 prints the pair, but roll stock is thermal paper, so a roll pair only suits a part that will not be wiped or clear-coated (CS-001 §7.5).

## 4. Which label stock: paper, 5522 polyester or the roll?

Anything that will see IPA, acetone or resin shall get a 5522 polyester label (or an overlaminated sheet label), never thermal roll stock (DK-2210, DK-1201). Roll stock is for shelves, bins, dry fabric and consumables only (CS-001 §7.2 stock rule).

Also called: label stock, Avery 5161, Avery 5522, polyester label, weatherproof label, thermal roll, Brother label, DK-2210.

| Stock | Use it for | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Avery 5161 paper sheet | Everyday dry things | CS-001 §5 |
| Avery 5522 polyester sheet | Untagged chemical containers, parts, molds, anything that sees IPA, acetone or resin | CS-001 §5, §7.2 |
| DK-2210 or DK-1201 thermal roll | Shelves, bins, dry fabric, consumables | CS-001 §5, §7.2 |

Thermal paper fades in UV, blackens with heat and smears under solvent (CS-001 §7.2). The Label button prints on the media this device is set to, under Reports → Label media. To print one record on a different stock, open the Print labels builder and pick it under Media (CS-001 §7.1).

## 5. What does a material lot label say?

The name and the ID, then one bold key line. For resin and hardener with no EH&S tag: the vendor lot number and opened date sit under the bold mix ratio and role; received date, expiry and shelf go in the footer (CS-001 §7.6).

Also called: lot label, material label, jug label, roll label, resin label, what's on the label.

| Class | What the label carries (CS-001 §7.6) |
|---|---|
| Part, test panel | The layup stack in CS-002 shorthand |
| Mold | The sealing record: sealed date, by whom, parts pulled, revision |
| Fabric | Vendor lot number on the detail line; received date and shelf in the footer. Dry fabric has no expiry field |
| Resin, hardener, consumable with no EH&S tag | Vendor lot and opened date under the mix ratio and role; received date, expiry and shelf in the footer |
| Board, remnant | Density in the key line; dimensions and quantity; the mold it came off and its shelf |
| Storage location | STORAGE; site and kind, and FLAMMABLES OK when the shelf is rated |

A container wearing an EH&S tag gets no FEB label at all (CS-001 §7.2).

The vendor lot and expiry print from whatever the record holds. If ✨ Read label filled them, check them against the container before you print (CS-001 §7.6).

An R&D build carries `R&D` in a box on the ID line. It means not a season deliverable, not scrap (CS-001 §7.6).

## 6. Chemical containers and the UC EH&S tag

A chemical container wearing a UC EH&S tag gets no FEB label. The campus tag is its label: record the tag in the lot's EH&S barcode field so scanning it opens the FEB record (CS-001 §7.11).

Also called: EH&S tag, UC tag, RSS tag, campus barcode, Data Matrix, chemical tag.

1. At receiving, scan or type each container's tag into the EH&S tag cell of its line on the Receiving desk (CS-001 §7.11 step 1; QR-006).
2. For a container already on a shelf, open its record, press Edit, and press the scan button beside EH&S barcode, or type the code.
3. If the face of the tag is scuffed, type the twelve characters printed down its right edge. If two containers share those twelve, the app asks for more of the code (CS-001 §7.11 step 3).
4. One tag, one container. The app refuses a tag another record already wears; fix the wrong record.
5. A code that is not 24 characters saves with a warning. Check it against the tag.
6. A chemical container with no tag yet gets a 5522 FEB label. When campus tags it, record the tag and leave the FEB label on (CS-001 §7.11 step 7).

Fabric lots have no EH&S barcode field: dry fabric is not in the campus chemical system (CS-001 §7.11).

## 7. No label printer, or the printer is broken

Print the label sheet on plain paper from the app, cut it on the guillotine, and overlaminate with 3 in clear packing tape overlapping the label by at least 6 mm on all four sides (CS-001 §7.8). This is the documented path, not a degraded one.

Also called: no printer, printer broken, printer not showing, overlaminate, packing tape label, paint marker.

1. Print the label sheet on plain paper from the app.
2. Cut on the guillotine, on the crop marks. Not with scissors.
3. Overlaminate with 3 in clear packing tape, overlapping the label by at least 6 mm on all four sides.
4. Burnish hard with a thumbnail.

Tape flush to the label edge lets solvent wick under, and it delaminates in a week (CS-001 §7.8). For anything tag-able, a laminating pouch on a zip tie seals all four edges; punch through the sealed margin, never the paper.

When the printer breaks, in order (CS-001 §7.9):

1. Use the spare roll taped to the printer. Most "broken printer" reports are an empty or jammed roll, or a printer off the shed wifi.
2. Print Avery 5161 on the office laser through the app, with the overlaminate above.
3. Paint marker on masking tape. Never scannable, acceptable for 72 hours.

## 8. Labeling a mixed pot or an opened container

Never print a label for a mixed pot. Write on the cup with tape and marker: product, time mixed (HHMM, 24 h) and initials, plus batch mass where CS-004 §7.3 requires it (CS-001 §7.7).

Also called: labeling, labelling; pot label, mixing cup, mixed resin, cup label, opened sticker, write-on tape.

A pot of XCR has an 8 minute pot life at 20 °C (CS-001 §7.7, from CS-004 §7.3). Standing at a printer burns that time. Write it like this (CS-001 §7.7):

```
IN2 2142 JS          product · time mixed (HHMM, 24 h) · initials
XCR 0906 SS 140g     add batch mass where CS-004 §7.3 requires it
```

Anything unlabelled and curing is treated as hot waste (CS-001 §7.7, CS-008).

Anything opened that has no lot record gets an opened sticker: `OPEN 2026-10-14 JS` (CS-001 §7.7). A container that has a lot record does not need one: press Open on its page and the app stamps the Opened date (CS-014 §7.8).

Use an oil-based paint marker for every hand mark, never a Sharpie. A standard Sharpie dissolves in IPA, and the process wipes with IPA at every stage (CS-001 §5).
