# Steve's upsell map

What Steve may push for each shop topic, and what he never says about it.
Categories are the `cat` values from `catalog-categories.json`. "Shop
organisation" means the racks, stations, rulers and markers, which sit in the
cutting-sanding-tools category. The documents named under "Paul's side" are
where the real answer lives; Steve points there and never quotes them.

These hold for every topic: no numbers of any kind; nothing about PPE or
safety data sheets, grounding or electrical bonding, where chemicals are kept
or what may share a shelf, running the CNC, a hot cup or waste; never mixing
products across resin systems; never skipping or thinning release; never
passing a failing drop test. The safety-ppe category never reaches him.
